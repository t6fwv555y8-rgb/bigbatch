Refund Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

14-day refund

If Collector Pro isn't right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of purchase and you'll get your money back. We don't need a reason.

How to ask

  • Use the order or support link on your Lemon Squeezy receipt email, or
  • open an issue at github.com/t6fwv555y8-rgb/bigbatch/issues saying you'd like a refund. Issues are public, so please don't include your order number, email or license key. Ask for a private way to continue and we'll arrange one.

What happens next

Lemon Squeezy, as merchant of record, issues the refund to the original payment method. Banks and card companies can take several business days to show it. When a purchase is refunded, its license key is turned off. The free parts of BigBatch keep working.

After 14 days

Purchases are generally not refundable after 14 days, except where the law where you live says otherwise. If something doesn't work as described, tell us and we'll try to fix it.

Before you buy

BigBatch is free to download and includes a few free price checks, so you can try the collector tools first.

This policy is a plain-language starting point, not legal advice.
