Questions

Does it work on my Mac?

Yes, on Intel and Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 13 or newer. The first time you open it, macOS will warn that it isn't from the App Store: right-click BigBatch, choose Open, then confirm. You only do this once.

Is it safe? What does it remember?

Your searches, library and collection are stored only on your own Mac. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to send a help report yourself. You can switch search memory off or clear it any time.

How do the price checks work?

BigBatch looks up the lowest current asking price on Discogs for each item and applies a rough condition factor. It's a guide, not an appraisal: fees and shipping come out of what you keep, so compare with eBay's sold listings before you list anything.

Can it save music or video?

BigBatch can save audio and video that you have the right to keep: your own uploads, Creative Commons and public-domain material, and anything you have permission to save. You're responsible for following copyright law and each site's terms of service.

How does the AI playlist maker work?

Torta works in two modes. Quick mode matches your words to the track names in your library, entirely on your Mac. If you paste your own Claude API key in Options, Torta uses it for smarter mixes; in that mode your request and your track titles are sent to Anthropic using your key, and you pay for your own usage. The key is kept in your Mac's Keychain.

Does BigBatch work with iPods?

It can prepare iPod-friendly files and copy them to Rockbox iPods, or add them to a playlist in the Music app so you can sync a stock iPod. This part is new, so tell us if your model doesn't behave.

What's your refund policy?

Collector Pro has a 14-day refund window, no questions asked. See the Refund Policy.

Where do I get help?

Open an issue on our support page. Issues are public, so please don't include personal information, order numbers or license keys. You can also use the Help tab inside the app to build a diagnostic report.

Something broke. Now what?

Open the Help tab. Run Check tests the app's tools, and Send Help Report bundles a screenshot and diagnostics into a zip you can send to us. Nothing is sent automatically.
