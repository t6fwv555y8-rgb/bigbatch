Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Short version: BigBatch has no accounts and no analytics. What you search for, your library, grades, notes, hunting list and collection stay on your own Mac. Below is exactly what does leave your Mac, and when.

Stored only on your Mac

  • Your library, grades, categories and notes
  • Your hunting list (what you search for), which you can switch off or clear at any time
  • Your collection list and price results
  • The artists and channels you choose to follow, and which tracks you saved from each. Following is optional and is never sent anywhere.
  • Settings, your chosen skin, and the Tortas you have caught
  • Optional keys you add yourself (a Discogs token, a Claude API key). The Claude key is kept in your Mac's Keychain.

What leaves your Mac

  • Searching and saving media. When you search or save something, BigBatch contacts the site involved (for example YouTube) from your Mac, like a browser would. That site sees your IP address and request under its own privacy policy.
  • Opening YouTube pages. The Open on YouTube, Watch & Like and Subscribe buttons just open the YouTube page in your browser. You watch, like or subscribe on YouTube yourself. BigBatch never likes, subscribes or registers views for you, and it never signs in to your YouTube account.
  • Price checks. If you use price checks, BigBatch sends the item names you listed to Discogs, using your own Discogs token.
  • Collector Pro license. Activating or re-checking a key sends the key, an activation ID and your Mac's device name to Lemon Squeezy, which runs the license service. The device name is the name your Mac uses on the network, so it may contain your name.
  • Torta playlists with AI (optional). Only if you add your own Claude API key, BigBatch sends your request, your track titles and your grades (never your notes) to Anthropic using your key. Without a key, Torta works on your Mac only.
  • Buy and merch links. Clicking a link opens your browser on that store. Some eBay or Amazon links include our affiliate tag so we earn a small commission if you buy. The app shows a disclosure when they do.
  • Support counts (optional, off by default). The first time you click a buy link or follow an artist, BigBatch asks whether you want to share an anonymous count. If you say yes, it sends only the artist name and the kind of action (buy-link click or follow) to the Artist Support Board. No IP address, device ID or account is stored with it, and you can switch it off in Options at any time. These counts are not download counts.
  • Help reports. The Help tab can build a zip with a screenshot of the BigBatch window, diagnostics and a recent error log. It is saved on your Mac. It only goes anywhere if you choose to send it.

Buying Collector Pro

Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record. It collects your payment details, email, country and tax information under its own privacy policy. We receive the order details we need to support you, such as a confirmation that a purchase happened. We never see your card number.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor information such as IP addresses under its own policy. We don't use cookies or analytics. If you pick a skin on the site, your choice is saved in your own browser's local storage, and never sent anywhere.

Keeping and deleting data

Because your data lives on your Mac, you control it: uninstall BigBatch and delete the folder ~/Library/Application Support/YouTubeToMP4 to remove its saved data. To ask about order data held for Collector Pro, open an issue (see below), and don't include personal information in it.

Children

BigBatch is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Questions: open an issue at github.com/t6fwv555y8-rgb/bigbatch/issues (public, so no personal information) and ask for a private way to continue.

This policy is a plain-language starting point, not legal advice.
